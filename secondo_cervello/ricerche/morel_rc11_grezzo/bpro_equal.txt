Equal Rights of Space
Project details
Programme
Research Cluster
RC11
Award
B-Pro Urban Design Silver Medal

In this revolutionary age of technology, the necessity of the existence of central business districts (CBDs), the architectural clusters occupying the most central spaces in cities that bring together the city’s elite workers in one location for productive activities, is constantly being debated. 'Equal Rights of Space' seeks to overturn and reorganise the traditional spatial evaluation system to system to inform the re-organisation of space. This would distribute the benefits of the CBD, which occupies the best space in the city and has the best public and transport facilities, to the whole city. It is a decentralisation based on egalitarianism, giving every citizen equal access to space in this information age.




This study began with broad spatial and socio-economic research and evaluation of La Défense. The results of different evaluations were combined through a series of experiments and studies on design concepts, urban planning, transport planning and the study of building monoliths. Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning technologies aid in the evaluation.
