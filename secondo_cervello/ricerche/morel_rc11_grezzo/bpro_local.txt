Refreshment of the Local
Project details
Programme
Research Cluster
RC11

Due to the boom of the Internet Economy and the impact of Covid-19, it seems that central business districts (CBDs) will face a heavy recession in the near future, especially for sub-CBDs. Through the analysis of local citizens’ lives, the "worst" areas were identified and then re-organised based on the segments of citizens’ activities. Through these transformations, the project aims to revitalise CBDs into more diverse and interesting places in order to attract the public.




The relationship between time and space was deeply considered in order to establish a CBD evaluation system. By connecting dynamic time intervals through static spatial paths, it is anticipated that different people in the venue can reach a space that better meets their requirements at different time periods to carry out corresponding activities. Artificial Intelligence (AI) makes this process more efficient and improves user satisfaction. The goal is to gradually replace the single office space with locals' 'daily life space' to make CBDs evolve to 'Refresh the Local'.
