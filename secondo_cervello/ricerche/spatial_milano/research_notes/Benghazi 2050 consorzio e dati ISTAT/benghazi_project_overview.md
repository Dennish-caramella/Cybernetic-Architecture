# Benghazi 2050 (ZHA-led) - project overview

Note: all sources are press/trade coverage of the consortium's own submission (press-release claims). No independent evaluation, no jury result, no ZHA.com page, and no hub URL found. The YouTube page returned only its title.

## Who commissioned the plan; formal name and framing

### Takeaway
The ZHA-led "Benghazi 2050: Next Generation City Plan" was a consortium entry to the Global Competitive Urban Planning Initiative - Benghazi (GCUPI-B), run by Libya's Fund for Development and Reconstruction (FDRL). Sources conflict on a "client": ArchDaily and e-architect name A&O TEC (consortium member), while the competition organiser is FDRL. It is a competition submission/concept, not a confirmed commissioned or adopted plan.

### Cited Findings
- GCUPI-B was launched under the mandate of FDRL; supervised by FDRL General Director Eng. Belkasem Hiftar (also spelled Belgasem Khalifa Haftar); partner is the Urban Planning Authority; described as the "world's first open international competitive process" for concept masterplans for Benghazi — [Zawya/TradeArabia](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/north-africa/benghazi-transformation-initiative-invites-firms-experts-to-join-key-event-415015); [GCUPI-B site](https://urbanbenghazi.org/)
- Official aim: integrated master plan for Benghazi 2050 (urban, economic, social, environmental), a smart, sustainable, globally competitive city and trade and logistics hub; focus on the Central Business District — [GCUPI-B site](https://urbanbenghazi.org/)
- Framing: flagship FDRL project to make Benghazi an "integrated international economic capital", trade/logistics hub linked to markets across three continents — [Zawya/TradeArabia](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/north-africa/benghazi-transformation-initiative-invites-firms-experts-to-join-key-event-415015)
- Evaluation: multidisciplinary technical committee (members unnamed); criteria creativity, cultural responsibility, urban efficiency, economic viability — [GCUPI-B site](https://urbanbenghazi.org/)
- Formal project name: "Benghazi 2050: Next Generation City Plan"; ZHA lead — [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1185260/zha-led-team-reveals-vision-and-digital-planning-hub-for-benghazis-2050-city-plan-in-libya); [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/libya/benghazi-2050-next-generation-city-plan-libya)
- "Client: A&O TEC" (German engineering, consulting and trading firm) per ArchDaily; e-architect credits A&O TEC as client, with contacts Oday Mosalam, Karim Assal, Yossef Assaf, Deepti Singh — [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1185260/zha-led-team-reveals-vision-and-digital-planning-hub-for-benghazis-2050-city-plan-in-libya); [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/libya/benghazi-2050-next-generation-city-plan-libya). Designboom, illustrarch, Parametric, africa.com name no client — [Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/zha-benghazi-libya-urban-growth-digital-planning-hub/)
- Consortium (7 firms): ZHA, A&O TEC, Prior+Partners (regional/urban planning, economic strategy, governance; also exhibition graphics, website design), Homa (land use, built form, socioeconomic), Buro Happold (natural environment, utilities), Systematica (transport, mobility), Fichtner (energy, infrastructure). ZHA: spatial vision, scenarios, hub interface and technology integration. Also Silicon Shore (web hosting/deployment), RJModels (models), Khalifa Holding Group (booth construction) — [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/libya/benghazi-2050-next-generation-city-plan-libya); [Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/zha-benghazi-libya-urban-growth-digital-planning-hub/); [Parametric](https://parametric-architecture.com/?p=186587)

### Inferences
- A&O TEC likely acted as the consortium's contracting/lead entity (the "client" credit in e-architect/ArchDaily), not the Libyan commissioning body; FDRL is the competition owner. Treat "commissioned by FDRL" as unconfirmed; "submitted to an FDRL-run competition" is supported.

## Deliverables and the "Foundations study"

### Takeaway
Two public headline products: Vision and Spatial Strategy, and the Benghazi 2050 Digital Planning Hub. e-architect adds a Baseline Study as a stage. No source defines a "Foundations study"; the term appears only in the YouTube video title.

### Cited Findings
- e-architect lists: (1) Baseline Study (existing urban, socioeconomic, environmental, infrastructure conditions), (2) Vision and Spatial Strategy, (3) Digital Planning Hub (shared evidence base/digital twin), (4) combined Next Generation City Plan, (5) exhibition design — [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/libya/benghazi-2050-next-generation-city-plan-libya)

- Hub contents: GIS/geospatial data, 3D urban models, population and land-use data, transport and infrastructure networks, environmental resilience analysis, ongoing/planned developments and scenarios; basis for a digital twin updated over time; used to test mobility, water, heritage, growth impacts — [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1185260/zha-led-team-reveals-vision-and-digital-planning-hub-for-benghazis-2050-city-plan-in-libya); [Parametric](https://parametric-architecture.com/?p=186587)

## Vision and spatial strategy for 2050

### Takeaway
A flexible coordination framework (not a fixed masterplan): connect, restore, adapt. Links historic core and waterfront to growing neighbourhoods via transit and pedestrian routes, adds employment/knowledge districts, and uses nature zones for water and ecology.

### Cited Findings
- Principles: "connecting rather than fragmenting, adaptable rather than fixed, restoration rather than demolition" — [e-architect](https://www.e-architect.com/libya/benghazi-2050-next-generation-city-plan-libya); [illustrarch](https://illustrarch.com/architecture-news/116073-zha-led-consortium-presents-benghazi-2050.html)
- Starts from analysis of urban character and collective memory — [Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/zha-benghazi-libya-urban-growth-digital-planning-hub/)
- Reconnect historic centre and shoreline with residential neighbourhoods and metropolitan centres via public transport and pedestrian corridors — [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1185260/zha-led-team-reveals-vision-and-digital-planning-hub-for-benghazis-2050-city-plan-in-libya)
- New employment and knowledge/innovation districts; landscape zones as water management and ecological infrastructure — [africa.com](https://africa.com/arts-culture-society/zha-led-team-unveils-benghazis-2050-urban-vision); [Parametric](https://parametric-architecture.com/?p=186587)
- Stated goal: "a more connected, livable, and resilient city" — [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/1185260/zha-led-team-reveals-vision-and-digital-planning-hub-for-benghazis-2050-city-plan-in-libya)
- Context: rapid expansion and chaotic sprawl after years of war — [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/world/africa/stop-building-libyas-benghazi-sees-chaotic-urban-sprawl); [Digital Journal](https://www.digitaljournal.com/article/after-years-of-war-libyas-benghazi-a-chaotic-urban-sprawl/) (seen as search results only, not fetched)
