## Methods, tools, outputs

### Takeaway
Methods are stated at project level only, not per firm. Systematica's Benghazi page is a separate, earlier project.

### Cited Findings
- Process combines "baseline research, growth scenarios, spatial planning and computational tools" — [Illustrarch](https://illustrarch.com/architecture-news/116073-zha-led-consortium-presents-benghazi-2050.html)
- Hub datasets: GIS, 3D urban models, population and land-use data, transport networks, infrastructure, environmental resilience studies, ongoing developments, future scenarios; shared evidence base / digital twin updated as data arrive — [Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/zha-benghazi-libya-urban-growth-digital-planning-hub/)
- "Evidence-based spatial model" on social patterns, natural systems, built environment; data queryable across land use, movement, ecology, heritage; framework like "generative constraints"; "living digital twin" — [Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/?p=186587)
- Strategy content: transit and pedestrian links between historic core, waterfront, neighborhoods, metropolitan centres; employment/knowledge districts; nature zones for water management and ecology — [Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/zha-benghazi-libya-urban-growth-digital-planning-hub/); [Illustrarch](https://illustrarch.com/architecture-news/116073-zha-led-consortium-presents-benghazi-2050.html)
- Systematica's earlier Benghazi Urban Mobility Plan: client ECOU, 2008-2010, three phases (data collection; wide-area highway assignment model of private traffic; assessment); outputs: new CBD for Cyrenaica, CBD-centred public transport, light metro and BRT lines, mobility demand forecast to double — [Systematica](https://www.systematica.net/project/benghazi-mobility-master-plan/) (page inconsistent on line counts)

### Inferences
- Hub "transport networks" layer plausibly from Systematica; "environmental resilience studies" from Buro Happold (INFERRED from workstreams, not stated).
- Systematica's 2008-10 data may have informed 2050 work (speculation; no source).

### Gaps
- No per-firm method/tool named for Benghazi 2050. No population/employment projection method, transport model, or water study named.

## Client-side counterpart and stakeholder use of the hub

### Takeaway
No source names the client or commissioning authority. Hub is for authorities, communities, planners, designers, investors.

### Cited Findings
- ZHA quote: hub is "a living environment for collaboration between authorities, local communities, planners, designers and investors"; proposals assessed against wider urban context — [Illustrarch](https://illustrarch.com/architecture-news/116073-zha-led-consortium-presents-benghazi-2050.html); [Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/zha-benghazi-libya-urban-growth-digital-planning-hub/)
- Users: planners, officials, investors, community representatives; test effects on mobility, water, heritage, growth before construction; Benghazi "actively discussing its next phase of reconstruction and growth" — [Parametric Architecture](https://parametric-architecture.com/?p=186587)
- Earlier unrelated mobility plan client: ECOU — [Systematica](https://www.systematica.net/project/benghazi-mobility-master-plan/)

### Inferences
- Do not assume ECOU is the 2050 client; sources do not link them.

### Gaps
- Client, contract, funding, adoption status, hub access rules, hub URL: not found. Homa's LinkedIn post (reportedly calling the hub a shared environment for authorities) was not retrieved.
