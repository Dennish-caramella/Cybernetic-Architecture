**Reading.** Interpret the labels with the effect sizes, not alone:
- The *placement* indices (poor share, foreign share, net income, residual income, concentration in high-ERP NIL) measure **where** the land sits relative to deprivation. They are the closest answer to 'does PGT land sit where poor people already are'.
- The *segregation* indices (ΔDI, Δisolation) measure the **change** in NIL-level sorting if the land were built with σ = 0.5 low-income occupancy. Milan's simulated ERP-eligible share is 0.46, close to σ, so new mixed housing barely moves DI anywhere (all ΔDI ≈ 10⁻⁴–10⁻³). A 'risk' label therefore means 'reduces segregation less than matched alternatives would', not 'creates a ghetto'. The σ sensitivity (σ = 1, all-social) is the stress case.

## 2. Pre-registered decision rule

> PRE-REGISTERED 2026-10-27, before any audit run. For each designated set G in {AT, AMB_DEG, ERP} and each null N in {size (area-decile + capacity +-5 %), ring (area-decile x centre-distance quintile + capacity +-5 %)}: one-sided empirical p in the harm direction, p = (1 + #{null at least as harmful as G}) / (R + 1), R = 999. Benjamini-Hochberg at q = 0.05 over the 4 sets x 6 indices (dDI, dIso, dep_poor, dep_resid, conc_q5, opp) separately within each null. SEGREGATION-RISK if (dDI or dIso is BH-significant) in BOTH nulls. REAL-POTENTIAL if served demand per EUR (D_res_decent, else D_tau) >= null median AND opportunity index >= null median, in BOTH nulls. Label = risk / potential / both / neither. RIG is reported as context, not labelled.

Written in `lab/model/config.py` before the first audit run. Unchanged afterwards. Null draws R = 999; capacity matched within ±5 % (match rate per set in `audit_indices.csv`). Disclosure: one trial run preceded the published one. It exposed a code bug in the verdict lookup (`Q.index` instead of the index column), which mislabelled AT as 'neither'. The bug was fixed; the rule text, indices and thresholds were not changed. Runtime settings (MILP pool 8/NIL, 30 s limit, export simplification 1 m) were changed after that trial run. Review 2 then changed the null pool from non-designated sites only to all candidate sites incl. G (calibration fix; it typically makes p larger) and re-ran.

**All tests are one-sided** in the harm direction stated in `config.AUDIT['bh_indices']` (+ = larger is worse; − for residual income and opportunity). The BH family is the stated 4 sets (AT, AMB_DEG, ERP, RIG) × 6 indices, per null; only AT, AMB_DEG and ERP get labels.

**Disclosed post-hoc specification changes (after review 2; the rule text is unchanged):**

- conc_q5 now uses the top quintile of p_erp only (the srv_erp arm made it partly tautological for ERP); the old OR definition is reported as conc_q5_or.
- Placement indices (poor, for_, net_m, resid, q5 membership) use a household floor: NIL with < 500 households take the city mean (their income is CAP-imputed).
- The null pool is all candidate land including G (permutation null), not non-designated land only (review 2 calibration fix).
- The BH family is now the stated 4 sets (AT, AMB_DEG, ERP, RIG) x 6 indices; the first runs used 3 sets.
- All p-values are one-sided in the harm direction (review1 said two-sided; config said one-sided). Two-sided p are in audit_indices.csv.
- NIL-block permutation null added as a co-primary robustness check (reported next to the label, not part of the rule).

## 8. Data status and caveats

**Real data:** cadastral parcels and DBT volumes, PGT 2030 layers (AT, ex-AMB_DEG, srv_erp, rig), DUSAF, census-2021 hex cells, MEF IRPEF 2024 by CAP, OMI 2019–2024 rents, NIL indicators (core.json), ISTAT absolute-poverty threshold (A, to verify).
**Assumptions:** FAR 1.0, yield 0.7, 80 m²/dwelling, cost model (A), τ 0.3, basket 1.5× ISTAT, renter share 0.30, σ 0.5, κ 0.03/0.015, λ 1.5 km, adjacency buffer 2.0 m, srv_erp land at 1 €/m².
**Synthetic:** archetype weight distributions (B), the synthetic city in tests; simulated households are a model of NIL distributions, not records.
**Limits:** (1) deprivation is NIL-ecological (88 NIL, income only at 38 CAP) — within-NIL placement is invisible except via C's modelled hex pressure; (2) the DI/isolation use the ERP-eligible share as 'low income'; (3) no ownership, constraints, heritage or real PGT indices — capacity is notional; (4) AMB_DEG has only 5 sites: low power, a 'neither' there is uninformative; (5) designated sets are evaluated as if fully built at FAR 1.0; (6) Euclidean distances; (7) served demand saturates for large sets (capacity ≫ D_res_decent), so served per € mostly tracks cost; served D_tau is reported too; (8) the PU score from C is not used anywhere (biased by design).
